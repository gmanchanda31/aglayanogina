<p><DropCap>T</DropCap>he celestial mines lie a million meters above sea level,</p>

<p>and tenderness is extracted there.</p>

<p>In the desperate search for tenderness,</p>

<p>first the skin on my knees and stomach scrapes against underwater reefs,</p>

<p>my skin stings a little from the saltwater,</p>

<p>and then I break against the coastal rocks.</p>

<p>And somewhere between the scratches and the stones,</p>

<p>I meet you every time.</p>

<p>Maybe you’re the only one who knows the true coordinates of the celestial mines.</p>

<p>The rocks lean over the water with jagged stone elbows.</p>

<p>At the tips of these elbows, the pine forest watches silently</p>

<p>as what’s left of me settles slowly onto the seaweed and sand after we’ve met.</p>

<p>Slowly — like dust drifting in a beam of backlight,</p>

<p>like atoms of weightlessness suspended in gravityless space.</p>

<p>I freeze.</p>

<p>Hold my breath.</p>

<p>Uno dos tres cuatro.</p>

<p>Then, with the next storm, I rise to the surface again.</p>

<p>The cycle repeats:</p>

<p>reefs / stomach / salt / you / celestial / mines / sharp impact / rock-elbows / pines / I’m gone.</p>

<p>For some reason, this autumn I crave, more than ever, to sit on your lap.</p>

<p>To sit in a chair on some wooden terrace wrapped in grapevines.</p>

<p>To drink tea from the same cup</p>

<p>and feel how my collarbones fit perfectly beneath the curve of your fingers.</p>

<p>How my cheekbones continue the lines of your temples.</p>

<p>How my breasts nest perfectly into the shape of your palms.</p>

<p>To watch how our hair tangles in the wind like stage curtains,</p>

<p>behind which my lips extend the line of your lips.</p>

<p>This happens only once every million years —</p>

<p>when two hunters of tenderness meet.</p>

<p>I take a running start and fall into you with immense force,</p>

<p>straight from the sky, like the first drops of rain,</p>

<p>and you, like warm steaming earth, catch me</p>

<p>and drink me in greedily.</p>

<p>Together we exist in a space of pure gravity,</p>

<p>dizzy with tenderness,</p>

<p>our ears popping like they do on a plane.</p>

<p>And there, a million meters above sea level,</p>

<p>in a zone of heightened turbulence,</p>

<p>we extract tenderness from the celestial mines.</p>

<p>We stuff our pockets with it and carry it in our bare hands,</p>

<p>breathe it into our lungs along with oxygen,</p>

<p>never getting enough.</p>

<p>Tenderness — desperately sought, and desperately wanting.</p>

<p>My cheekbones continue the line of your temples.</p>

<p>You’ll text me that you miss me</p>

<p>when you step out to buy bread.</p>

<p>And I’ll come out barefoot to meet you.</p>

<p>uno — the bread will be hot and fierce, like you, like the earth, like the sun.</p>

<p>dos — it will be summer, and the rain will begin.</p>

<p>tres — the scratches from the salt will burn on my knees and stomach.</p>

<p>cuatro — and every time, I’ll crash even harder.</p>
